UPLOAD ONLY THESE 5 FILES TO THE ROOT OF YOUR GITHUB REPOSITORY:

1. main.py
2. planner.sqlite3
3. requirements.txt
4. Dockerfile
5. render.yaml

Do not put them inside app/, scripts/ or seed/.
They must appear directly on the first page of the repository.

For the cleanest result, you can leave your old files there, but these 5 must be in the root.

Then in Render:
- Root Directory: blank
- Manual Deploy -> Deploy latest commit

The Dockerfile runs:
uvicorn main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port $PORT
